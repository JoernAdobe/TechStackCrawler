## Rules for choosing products in s14

1. 4–6 products, ranked by deal-size potential × timing proximity (see `workflow/research-guide.md`, s14).
2. Each product needs one cited client fact, one measurable outcome and, where available, a timing signal (replatform, agency review, regulatory deadline, fiscal year start).
3. Do not pad with products that have no supporting signal in s01–s13 — fewer, well-evidenced recommendations beat a long list.
4. Do not claim capabilities, pricing, customer wins or partnerships that you have not verified from an Adobe source.
5. **Check what the client already runs.** If page inspection or reliable sources show the client already uses an Adobe product (for example AEM), say so and frame the recommendation as extending or measuring it — never as a first purchase.
6. Write each recommendation as one flowing paragraph under a title `N. Adobe Product (detail) — headline benefit` (see `workflow/standard-sections.md`), linking earlier sections by name.
5. If this table is missing a product you want to recommend, verify its name and scope on adobe.com first and cite that page.
